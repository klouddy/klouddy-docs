# Weeks 3-4: Prototype and CAD/Build

**Dates:** Sept 26 - Oct 9

These weeks move the team from ideas into real testing, partial builds, and early code integration. Students should be learning from the robot, not waiting for it to be perfect.

## EDP Stages

- Develop and Prototype Solutions
- Refine and Optimize

## What Students Should Do

- Prototype key mechanisms using fast, low-cost materials
- Compare prototype versions and record what changes improved performance
- Continue CAD for the selected robot layout
- Mount hardware onto the drive base
- Complete initial electrical wiring
- Begin basic autonomous setup, sensors, and control testing
- Add weekly documentation with photos, CAD screenshots, and design notes

## What This Looks Like in a Team Meeting

- Build a quick prototype before making a final part
- Test one change at a time when possible
- Take photos or short notes after each meaningful change
- Share progress across mechanical, electrical, and coding groups
- Decide what should be rebuilt, improved, or kept

## Suggested Student Outputs

- Prototype photos or sketches
- A short log of failures, changes, and lessons learned
- Updated CAD screenshots
- An initial wiring plan
- A first working hardware prototype or robot subsystem

## By the End of Week 4, Students Should Be Able To Say

- We tested real ideas, not just talked about them.
- We know at least one thing that failed and what we learned from it.
- We have a robot subsystem that is starting to work.
- We can explain why our current design is better than our first idea.

## Student Learning Focus

- Learn that early prototypes are allowed to fail
- Practice changing one thing at a time and observing the result
- Build the habit of documenting iterations, not just final answers

## Good Weekly Questions

- What did this prototype teach us?
- What worked, and what failed?
- What is the next revision we should test?
- Are we building something students can assemble and maintain reliably?

## Quick Team Checklist

- We built at least one real prototype.
- We documented changes from one version to the next.
- We started wiring and integrating real hardware.
- We can name our next test instead of just saying "keep working on it."

## Team Habit to Reinforce

Require every sub-team to share one thing that improved and one thing that still needs work at the end of each week.
